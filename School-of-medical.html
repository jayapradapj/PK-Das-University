About School of Health Sciences

The School of Health Sciences at PKDAS Deemed to be University stands as a premier center for allied healthcare and medical diagnostic education in Southern India. Grounded in the statutory curriculum benchmarks of the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions (NCAHP) and UGC guidelines, our school integrates deep biological sciences with rigorous hospital-based clinical training.

Our students benefit from daily clinical rotations at the PK DAS Institute of Medical Sciences & Hospital (PKDIMS) — a 750+ bedded super-specialty teaching hospital equipped with tertiary-care ICUs, 12 modern modular operating theatres, automated diagnostic pathology labs, and digital radiological suites. We groom empathetic, skilled medical technologists capable of thriving in demanding tertiary healthcare environments globally.
