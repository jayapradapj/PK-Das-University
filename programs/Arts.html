Programme Overview

The Four-Year B.A. Performing Arts (Honours) programme is a comprehensive and interdisciplinary undergraduate degree designed to nurture artistic excellence, creativity, cultural awareness, and professional competence in the field of performing arts. The programme provides students with rigorous training in performance, theory, history, aesthetics, production, and contemporary artistic practices.

The curriculum integrates traditional and modern approaches to Theatre, Dance, Music, Stagecraft, and Performance Studies, enabling students to develop technical proficiency, critical thinking, and artistic expression. Through classroom instruction, studio practice, workshops, productions, internships, and community engagement, students gain hands-on experience and industry exposure.

The programme is aligned with contemporary educational frameworks and industry requirements, preparing graduates for rewarding careers in Performing Arts, Media, Entertainment, Education, Cultural Organizations, and Creative Industries.

Teaching Methodology

Pedagogy & Practical Training

A dynamic delivery model combining academic theory with studio practice, stage recitals, capstone projects, and industry immersion.

01
Classroom Teaching

Rigorous intellectual grounding in performing arts history, aesthetics, Natyashastra principles, dramaturgy, and world artistic traditions.

02
Studio Practical Training

Specialized practical training in acoustically treated sound studios, dance practice halls, and black-box acting spaces with one-on-one guru mentorship.

03
Instructional Stage Recitals

Instructional learning through mandated performances across the 6th, 7th & 8th semesters, experiencing all aspects of live stagecraft.

04
Project-Based & Capstone

Hands-on industry internships in the 7th semester followed by a milestone 8th-semester Capstone project presented before an esteemed academic jury.

Live Performance Roadmap
On-Stage & Backstage
Semesters 1 to 5: Studio Lab sessions organized in tandem with core theory coursework.
Semesters 6, 7 & 8: Mandated live performances experiencing lighting, stage design, sound & performance.
Semester 7 & 8 (Honours): Industry internship + 8th-semester capstone presentation before domain experts.
Target Audience

Who Should Pursue This Programme?

Devised by keeping the emerging trends of the entertainment industry and the evolving needs of an artist in mind, this course is tailored for students who fully intend on pursuing a successful, prosperous career in the entertainment and creative arts sector.

A career in performing arts usually begins as a personal hobby in the formative years of a student’s life. This course aims at helping students harness this early-founded passion and convert it into a structured, commercially viable, and respected profession.

Turning Hobbies into Professional Artistry Transform unstructured musical, dance, or dramatic talents into polished, industry-presentable artistry.

Actor (Screen & Stage)

Actors dominate screens, big and small, all over the world. With the prowess to demonstrate emotion on demand along with interdisciplinary arts mastery, an acting career yields immense commercial acclaim and enduring social prominence.

Professional Dancer & Performer

Dancers use their mastery of musical rhythms to bring poetic expression to life. Renowned in cinema, theatrical dance dramas, and international festivals, professional dancers are sought after for global cultural tours and recitals.

Musical Theatre Performer

Tri-discipline artists blending singing, dancing, and dramatic acting to bring grand musical theatre productions to life. With the worldwide boom in musical theatre audiences, performers command high artistic and commercial prestige.

Dance, Music & Drama Therapist

Specialized clinical creative therapists utilizing music, dance movements, and drama therapy to help healthcare and rehabilitation patients express emotions, heal psychological trauma, and enhance mental well-being.

Theatre & Stage Director

Creative directors responsible for envisioning theatrical masterpieces, coaching actors, interpreting scripts, orchestrating lighting and sound design, and overseeing comprehensive production logistics.

Screenwriter & Playwright

Visionary writers crafting movie scripts, teleplays, dialogues, and theatrical texts from scratch. Screenwriters breathe life into memorable characters and lay the literary foundation for blockbuster cinema and streaming series.

Arts Administrator & Curator

Arts administrators plan, finance, organize, and market cultural events, festivals, art services, and exhibitions across major national theatres, heritage museums, cultural trusts, and international arts organizations.

Theatre Stage Manager

Key logistical and technical leaders who ensure live stage shows run flawlessly. Stage managers coordinate backstage crew, actor cues, props, set transitions, and technical execution through stellar interpersonal and networking skills.

Performing Arts Facilitator & Educator

Inspiring educators and artistic mentors working within premier universities, colleges, international schools, and fine arts academies, guiding the next generation of creative performers and cultural scholars.
