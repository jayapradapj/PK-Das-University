Programme Overview

The Bachelor of Anaesthesia and Operation Theatre Technology (BAOTT) at P K DAS Deemed to be University is a specialized School of Health Sciences programme that prepares students to become skilled professionals in surgical and perioperative healthcare. The programme provides comprehensive knowledge and practical training in operation theatre management, anaesthesia technology, patient monitoring, infection control, and emergency care.

Students receive hands-on clinical exposure in modern operation theatres and critical care settings, enabling them to work effectively alongside surgeons, anaesthesiologists, and healthcare teams to ensure safe and successful surgical procedures.

Covering Operation Theatre Setup, Anesthesia Equipment, Sterilization Protocols, and Critical Care, the Curriculum emphasizes absolute Accuracy and Patient Safety. Students receive rigorous clinical training to handle life-saving equipment and manage emergencies.

Course Objectives

01

Operation Theatre Preparation

Prepare and organise the operating theatre, including instruments, linen and equipment, to ensure readiness for surgical procedures.

02

Sterilisation & Infection Control

Apply standard sterilisation methods and aseptic techniques, and manage biomedical waste in accordance with established protocols.

03

Anaesthesia Equipment Management

Operate, maintain and troubleshoot anaesthesia machines, ventilators and airway devices with precision.

04

Patient Monitoring

Monitor and record vital parameters during procedures, and promptly report any deviations to the anaesthetist.

05

Anaesthesia Team Support

Assist the anaesthetist in induction, drug preparation, intubation and airway management.

06

Surgical Team Assistance

Position patients correctly, handle instruments efficiently and maintain accurate swab, needle and instrument counts.

07

Emergency & Resuscitation Support

Provide Basic Life Support and maintain emergency equipment in a state of constant readiness.

08

Post-Operative & Recovery Care

Ensure safe patient transfer and continuous monitoring during the recovery period.

09

Patient Safety & Documentation

Adhere to the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist and maintain accurate clinical and equipment records.

10

Professional Practice

Demonstrate effective teamwork, clear communication and adherence to ethical and professional standards.
