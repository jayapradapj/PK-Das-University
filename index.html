About PK DAS University

PK DAS Institute of Social Sciences, Health Sciences & Technology is a Deemed to be University established under Section 3 of the UGC Act, 1956.

The University aims to impart complete Indian education integrated with modern science and technology across disciplines including Arts, Science, Health Sciences, Technology, Heritage, Language and Literature.

Honouring Excellence

LifeTime Achievement Awards

Instituted in revered memory of our Founder Chairman, Late Shri P. K. Das, celebrating educational visionaries who dedicate their lives to shaping future generations.

The prestigious Shri P. K. Das Memorial Lifetime Achievement Award is a flagship pan-India recognition instituted by the Nehru Group of Institutions. Conferred annually, it honours eminent academicians, scientists, and educational leaders who have rendered more than 25 years of distinguished service in higher education across government, aided, and self-financing universities.
